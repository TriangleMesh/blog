### 🚀 Projects  

**Exam Generation & Analysis System**  
I designed and built a platform to make exam creation less tedious and more reliable. It uses semantic search to recommend similar questions, shuffles intelligently to keep tests fair, and runs on a microservice architecture for scalability. Along the way, I learned a lot about performance optimization and reduced compute costs dramatically.

**Flowboard – Project Management Web App**  
A collaboration tool built with React, Tailwind, and Appwrite, with a focus on usability and streamlined deployment.
